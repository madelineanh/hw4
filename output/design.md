# Problem 10: Campus Customs design

- Added a layered paper-and-rule texture, deep Yale-blue color system, and refined gold accents. The storefront feels more considered and memorable while keeping the page easy to scan.
- Elevated products with an on-image garment label, stronger card lift, image zoom, and a clear directional arrow. This makes browsing feel tactile and makes each card more obviously clickable.
- Added restrained motion to the hero architecture, header links, and product cards. The movement gives the shop energy without interrupting reading or shopping.
- Refined the Campus Concierge with a warm “live” cue, suggestion chips, and message bubbles. It makes the agent feel approachable and invites a shopper to start a useful conversation.

Together, the visual hierarchy emphasizes products and decisions first: browse, filter, ask, and open details. The styling stays calm enough for a real shop while giving Campus Customs a distinct point of view.
